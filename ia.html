Sift

One object, three piles, four screens.

A screenshot is the only thing the app is about. It ends up in the trash list, the archive list or Photos’ favorites; the queue is whatever is in none of them, newest first. Every screen, route and side effect on this page follows from that one rule. The written version is docs/knowledge/IA.md.

Proposed ADR-025 v0.1
01

Object model

One object the app is about, three memberships it can hold, and a queue that is computed rather than stored. A verdict is not a field on the screenshot; it is which list the screenshot is in.

Derivation rules

1 · unreviewed

Not in the trash list, not in the archive list, not favorited. The queue is every screenshot that is unreviewed, newest first; the front card is the newest unreviewed one.

2 · Trash wins on display

A screenshot in the trash list appears only in Trash, even if it is also favorited or archived. The app clears the other two when it trashes; the rule covers edits made in Photos afterwards.

3 · Library segments

Favorites is favorited and not trashed; Archive is in the archive list and not trashed. A screenshot can be in both segments and then appears in both.

4 · Counter

total is what the app can see, reviewed is total minus the queue. The header shows reviewed + 1 / total while a card is up, and total / total when the queue is empty.

Consequence. A screenshot hearted in Photos before the app ever saw it is already a Favorite and never enters the queue; un-hearting it later puts it back. The app owns no “reviewed” flag that could drift from what Photos shows. That is assumption A1 below.

03

Navigation

One navigation stack, depth at most two, and no tab bar. Covers and sheets sit on top; the Permission screen is not on the stack at all.

The stack

Review is the root. Trash and Library push from the Review header; Credits pushes from Library. Nothing pushes from Trash.

Covers and sheets

The Viewer is a full-screen cover over Trash or Library. The Purge-all sheet is a sheet over Trash. The limited-library picker and the two iOS dialogs are system-owned.

Permission is not on the stack

While authorization is unusable, Permission is the root; on grant, Review takes its place. If authorization is revoked in the background the stack is discarded and Permission returns in its denied state.

Back, and what does not exist

System back on pushes, swipe-down or close on the Viewer, “Keep them” on the sheet. No tab bar (ADR-011), no deep links, no URL scheme, no widgets, no Shortcuts in v1.

04

Evaluated at launch and again on every return to the foreground, so revocation, a return from Settings and a change to the limited selection all land in the right place without a tap.

Deliberately not stored

No “onboarding seen” flag: Permission appears whenever the status makes it necessary and never otherwise. No per-screenshot “reviewed” flag: derived. No copy of the favorite flag. No session log, no statistics.

After a reinstall

The trash list is gone, and because nothing was deleted from Photos the trashed screenshots simply return to the queue. The archive list is gone too, but the album is not: an empty store adopts an album titled with the brand name and seeds the list from its members.

08

External change

The app observes the library and reconciles on every change and on every return to the foreground. Because the queue is derived, most edits made in Photos need no special case; these are the ones with a visible effect.

Change made in PhotosEffect in the app
A screenshot is deleteddropped from the queue and both lists silently; if it was the front card the deck promotes without a verdict
A screenshot is takenjoins the queue by date, behind the front card; total rises; noScreenshots becomes reviewing
A screenshot is heartedleaves the queue; appears in Library · Favorites
A heart is removedreturns to the queue unless it is in the trash or archive list
A screenshot is removed from the archive album by handtreated as un-archiving: the entry is dropped, the screenshot returns to the queue
The archive album is deletedthe list is the truth: the album is recreated and repopulated on the next archive or launch
The limited selection changesthe interstitial shows on the next return to the foreground
Authorization is revokedPermission · denied; the stack is discarded
Access widens from limited to fullReview; the acknowledged selection count is cleared
Never under the thumb. A screenshot that arrives, leaves or changes membership mid-drag is applied at the next promoting, not during dragging or exiting.

10

Assumptions to confirm

Structural choices this architecture had to make that no ADR settles. Each is written as a rule above so the diagrams are complete; each is a one-line change if decided otherwise. Recorded as ADR-025, status Proposed.

A1

A pre-existing heart counts as reviewed

A screenshot hearted in Photos before the app ever saw it is a Favorite and never enters the queue. The alternative is an app-side “seen” set, which makes FAVE a no-op on an already-hearted card.

A2

The limited interstitial shows only when the selection changed

On first grant, and whenever the selected count differs from the one last acknowledged; otherwise limited access goes straight to Review. The alternative is showing it on every launch.

A3

Credits lives in the Library footer

Once access is granted the Permission footer is gone, so the Library footer carries the link. The alternatives are a header overflow menu on Review, or the Settings bundle.

A4

The archive list is the truth; the album mirrors it

A deleted album is recreated, a hand-removed member is un-archived, an empty store adopts an existing album. The alternative, the album as truth, loses every archive verdict if the user deletes the album.

Open, not assumed. Whether the Review card offers pinch-to-zoom. The design system gives zoom to the Viewer only; the card is the screenshot at card size.

Out of scope for v1
