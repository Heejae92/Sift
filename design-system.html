Sift

Swipe your screenshots into three piles.

The app shows one screenshot at a time, newest unreviewed first. Left trashes, right archives, up favourites — and one rewind undoes the last call. Everything on this page comes from the Swift token files; the block you are reading is the onboarding block, tomato with navy ink.

01

Principles

Global rules, in force on every screen: no borders (the focus ring is the one exception) · no emoji · no icon tiles · no color-only meaning · no dark mode.

The screenshot is the hero

The card is the product. Chrome stays out of its way: a counter, two icon buttons, a verdict row. No tab bar, no toolbars, no second layout system.

Three verbs, three colors, never color alone

Trash, Archive, Fave. Direction, glyph and word carry the meaning; hue is the third channel, so the system still works for a deuteranope and in a screenshot.

One queue, one step back

A single queue, newest unreviewed first, and exactly one level of undo. Deeper mistakes are handled by Trash restore and by moving items in Library.

Destruction is confirmed by the system

Verdicts are reversible and need no confirmation. Permanent deletion always ends in the iOS dialog; deleting everything adds an in-app sheet that names the count first. The size rides on the docked button that opens it, not in the sheet.

Saturation belongs to verdicts and blocks

Chrome is navy on white. The only saturated things on screen are the three verdict colors and the expressive blocks behind onboarding and empty states.

Typeset, don't draw

Illustration is one line of type on a color block: round brackets for the head, one letter from a world script for the face. No image assets, no mascots, nothing to redraw when a string changes.

Typography

Two voices. Forager Bold Overlap is the display face, at its single weight of 700 — wordmark, display, headline, title and stamp all sit on it. Pretendard is the text face — Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold. Eleven roles, mirroring DSTextRole. Ten bind to a Dynamic Type style through relativeTo:; stamp is the exception, returning a fixed-size font so its 44 pt cap cannot be scaled twice, with the call site owning @ScaledMetric.

The license note

Forager (Mark Simonson Studio) ships through Adobe Fonts, whose license covers web and desktop but not embedding in a mobile app. Until an app license is bought, the app declares the face and resolves it to Pretendard Bold at runtime — DSFont.display probes UIFont(name:"Forager-BoldOverlap") once per cut. This page loads it from Typekit kit blt4ith (family forager-overlap), which carries 400 and 700 — there is no heavier cut, so every display role is set at 700 rather than the 900 the previous face allowed. Without the kit the page renders the same Pretendard fallback, so nothing silently changes shape (ADR-003).

Caption is never a rung above body

Hierarchy moves size and weight in the same direction. A smaller-but-bolder line reads as no hierarchy at all, and at accessibility text sizes the two styles converge — so caption stays under body, never over it. Data digits (counter, sizes, dates, D-numbers) are tabular so the glyphs never jitter.

Spacing is 6 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 28 · 32, inherited verbatim from the previous system (ADR-018). Radius is not a fixed decoration: it tracks the element's inner padding.

Nested radius = outer − gap. A thumbnail (xs 6) sitting 4 pt inside a card corner of md 12 looks wrong at 12 and right at 8. The screenshot card is xl 24 because its inner padding is 24; the stamp on it is a pill because its own padding is 8×16. Radius follows padding, not taste.

Do — glyph on the surface

Emphasis comes from the glyph's own color, size and surrounding space. The icon sits directly on the surface.

Don't — filled icon tile

No colored square or rounded tile behind a glyph. A tile invents a surface the system does not have and competes with the verdict colors.

06

Elevation & Layering

Borders are forbidden; the focus ring is the only line the system draws. Surfaces separate by tint and by whitespace. Shadows exist only for things that float — the front card, a committed stamp, the verdict buttons, the toast, sheets — and are two soft layers at 10 % and 5 % of the ink.

Flat — the default

No shadow. This card separates from the panel behind it by tint alone, which is how every list row, chip and segment works.

Floating — --shadow-1

0 1px 3px shadowNear + 0 10px 24px -6px shadowFar, cast downward. DSShadow.floating.

Stacked — --shadow-stack

The same two layers cast upward, so a card reads as lying on the one below it. DSShadow.stack.

In SwiftUI every shadow goes through .dsShadow(_:), which applies compositingGroup() first — otherwise each child is shadowed separately and a card grows a halo.

07

Interaction & Motion

Only transform and opacity animate; transition: all is banned. Entries ease out, exits are faster, and every animation is gated on Reduce Motion through DSMotion.gated(_:reduce:). Press is scale(0.90) on every button — press one below.

Buttons

Primary is a navy pill with a white label — the accent is the ink. Secondary is surfaceRaised with ink. Destructive is tomato with a navy label at 4.61:1. A 16 pt bold label is not WCAG large text, which starts at 19 pt bold, so white on tomato at 3.51 would not clear the 4.5 bar. It always carries the number. Tab to any of them for the ink focus ring.

Swipe Deck

The product, running. This deck implements DSSwipe and DSMotion exactly as the app will: four 90° sectors with 6° of hysteresis, a commit at 120 pt or 800 pt/s, a velocity-derived throw, and promotion at 60 % of the exit. Drag a card, press a verdict button, or focus the phone and use ← → ↑ and Z.

What the gesture says

←TRASH — the app's own Trash. Reversible until you empty it.
→ARCHIVE — a Photos album you can find later.
↑FAVE — the Photos heart, set on the asset itself.
↓Dead zone — the card follows at 0.35 and springs back. Down means nothing on purpose.

Stamps are pills: TRASH top-right at +12°, ARCHIVE top-left at −12°, FAVE centred at 0°, each with the verdict glyph and the word in the display face. Their opacity ramp ends exactly at the commit distance, so a fully opaque stamp is the "release will commit" signal. Solid fill, not outline: screenshots are usually white and an outline vanishes — the fourth placeholder in this deck is pure white to prove it, and the fifth is near-black.

With Reduce Motion on, the throw is replaced by a cross-fade: the stamp holds for DSMotion.stampHoldReduced so the verdict is readable, then the card fades out over DSMotion.crossFade. Drag still moves the card one-to-one — direct manipulation is not decoration.

Sectors — θ = atan2(−dy, dx)

A sector only changes when θ crosses 6° into the neighbour, so a finger resting on a boundary cannot flicker between two verdicts.

A face is one line: a pair of round brackets in the display face for the head, and a single letter borrowed from a world script doing the eyes and the mouth at once. Checklist

Run this before a screen leaves your hands.

  • Every color comes from a token — no literal hex outside DesignSystem/, and no opacity used to invent a text color.
  • On a verdict main fill only the 32 pt stamp word and the glyphs use on; every smaller label is ink, and text on a soft tint is ink.
  • Nothing depends on the edge of fave.main — the yellow exemption is acknowledged wherever yellow appears.
  • No borders anywhere. The focus ring is the only line, and surfaces separate by tint and whitespace.
  • Shadows appear only on things that float — front card, committed stamp, verdict buttons, toast, sheets — and go through .dsShadow(_:).
  • Radius tracks the element's inner padding, and a nested radius is the outer radius minus the gap.
  • Type uses only the eleven DSTextRole roles — Forager Bold Overlap for display, Pretendard for text — and caption never sits above body.
  • Every hit target is at least 44 pt; the hit area is padded, never the glyph.
  • Every animation is gated on Reduce Motion through DSMotion.gated, and only transform and opacity animate.
  • VoiceOver reads the front card as one element, with custom actions in the order Trash → Archive → Favorite → Rewind last.
  • Every shipped Noun Project glyph has its CC BY line in DSIconCredits.entries, and no glyph sits inside a filled tile.
  • All four verification commands are green: ds_tokens.py contrast, ds_tokens.py emit-css, ds_tokens.py lint, typecheck-ds.sh.

This page is generated from the same Swift tokens the app compiles: the :root block at the top of its stylesheet is the verbatim output of python3 scripts/ds_tokens.py emit-css, and ds_tokens.py lint fails if it drifts.
